Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Mouse Gestures does not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any user data. All processing occurs entirely inside your local browser.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Info: No names, email addresses, or identifiers.
  • No Browsing History: No logging or inspection of URLs or page content.
  • No Keystroke or Gesture Logging: Mouse coordinates are processed in real time and discarded immediately.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: Zero third-party scripts, telemetry, or analytics.

Permissions Explained

  • sessions: Used solely to reopen the last closed tab when triggered by gesture.
  • storage: Used only to save your custom gesture bindings. If Chrome Sync is enabled, Chrome syncs these preferences across your devices.
  • Content Scripts: Matches HTTP/HTTPS pages to listen for mouse strokes while the right button is held. It never reads web page data.

Open Source

The full source code is publicly inspectable and auditable on GitHub.
